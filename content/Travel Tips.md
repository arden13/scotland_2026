Here's a list of best tips we've found from our previous trips to [[Scotland]].
# Before Going
- **Get an [Electronic Travel Authorization](https://www.gov.uk/eta/apply)**
	- **This is required to enter the UK**
	- Should cost £16
	- Apply via the apps listed on the page linked here
- **Make sure you have 6 months valid on your passport!**
	- you will be denied entry if your passport expires sooner
- Check with your phone provider about International Data
- Download WhatsApp for international texting (requires data connection)
- Purchase a Travel Adapter
	- recommend finding one with USB/USB-C built in so you don't need a separate phone charger
- Download Splitwise
	- great for sharing costs in a group!
# Getting Around Scotland
- Download GPS maps beforehand
	- many areas of Scotland are remote and you will not have reception
- In many towns in Scotland, the towns wind down after ~9:00 pm. You'll want to make sure you have food arranged for (or eaten!) by then.
- Dinner reservations are strongly recommended due to limited restaurants in smaller areas
- If you're traveling outside of the cities, you'll probably want to rent a car
