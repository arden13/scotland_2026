# Activities
- [Skara Brae](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/visit-a-place/places/skara-brae/)
	- Neolithic Village 
- [Maeshowe](https://www.secret-scotland.com/place/maeshowe)
	- A burial cairn
- [Stenness Standing Stones](https://www.secret-scotland.com/place/stenness-standing-stones)
- [Midhowe Chambered Cairn](https://www.secret-scotland.com/place/midhowe-chambered-cairn)
	- Large Burial Cairn
	- requires a ferry trip to get to it!
		- [Search Rousay to Tingwall](https://www.orkneyferries.co.uk/timetables)
			- approximately 15GBP with a car
- [Taversoe Tuick Chambered Cairn](https://www.secret-scotland.com/place/taversoe-tuick-chambered-cairn)
	- Spooky two story burial cairn
	- Requires a ferry to get to it
- [Old Man of Hoy](https://hoyorkney.com/attractions/hoy-geography/old-man-of-hoy/)
	- A scenic coastal walk [Alltrails link](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/scotland/orkney-islands/rackwick-to-old-man-of-hoy)
	- Requires a ferry
	- Make sure to look for it on the ferry to Stromness!
