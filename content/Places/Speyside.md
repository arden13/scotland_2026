One of the major regions of whiskey distilling in [[Scotland]] around the river Spey. The area is home to a lot of neat towns and pastoral views and is near the [[Cairngorms National Park]].

[Wikipedia link for the river Spey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Spey)
